Privacy Policy

The Car Lab ("we", "us") operates the The Car Lab app, a workshop management app used by our staff in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Last updated: November 2026.

Who the app is for

The app is for staff of The Car Lab only. Accounts are created by our administrator. Members of the public cannot register.

Information we store

To run the workshop the app stores: staff login details (name, email, role); customer details (name, mobile and WhatsApp number, email, TRN); vehicle details (make, model, year, plate, VIN, mileage); inspection, service job and service contract records, including notes and photos taken in the app; and generated PDF reports.

How we use it

We use this information only to carry out inspections, services and contracts, to send reports and reminders to customers, and to keep business records. We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Data is stored on our own secured server and is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Daily backups are kept. Access is limited by staff role.

Sharing

We share a report with a customer only when our staff send it to them, for example by WhatsApp. We do not share data with other third parties except where required by law.

Device permissions

The app may ask for camera and photo access to attach pictures to inspections, and uses the phone's share and call functions when staff contact customers. These are used only when staff choose to.

Keeping and deleting data

Records are kept for as long as needed for the business. A customer or staff member can ask us to correct or delete their information by contacting us below.
